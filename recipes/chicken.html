Chicken

Description

Crispy breaded chicken cutlets smothered in rich marinara sauce and melted mozzarella, delivering a satisfying crunch beneath bubbling cheese. It’s an Italian-American staple that pairs effortlessly with pasta or a light side salad.

Ingredients

  • Chicken breasts
  • flour
  • eggs

Steps

  1. Slice chicken breasts thinly
  2. Pan-fry cutlets in olive oil
  3. Transfer to a baking dish
