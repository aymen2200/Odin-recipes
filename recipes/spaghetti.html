Spaghetti

Description

A rich, velvety Roman pasta dish made without heavy cream, relying on eggs and aged cheese to create a glossy, flavorful sauce. Crispy cured pork adds a salty crunch that balances the silky noodles and fresh black pepper.

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • guanciale
  • egg yolks
  • Pecorino Romano cheese
  • salt

Steps

  1. Boil spaghetti
  2. Crisp diced guanciale in a skillet over medium heat
  3. Toss hot drained pasta in the skillet
